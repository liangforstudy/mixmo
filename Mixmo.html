The Curious Gait of a Three-Legged Sergal

Drag to turn him about, pinch to look closer, slide two fingers to move the view.

Mode:

Game: steer him with the keys. Animation player: pick animations, turns and the timeline. Dev: also the advanced settings (gait sliders, leg placement, footfall chart, sergal parts, save & load, bone and debug tools).

Game. WASD to run, hold Shift to walk; the arrow keys walk (while paused, ← → step frames); on the robot, Ctrl = his in-between form. On a phone, drag in the left half of the view: a joystick appears under your thumb; push a little to walk, all the way to run. Up = away from the camera. To look around, drag the right half on a phone, or anywhere with a mouse. Psst: try spinning the joystick round.

Ceiling: he crouches, jumps, and the feet nearest the ceiling grab it first. Out of reach, he jumps, reaches up and lands again.

Runs on 4, 5 or 6 legs, to and from the ceiling:

End over end: he turns head over tail, so his ends change places. Roll belly-up: he rolls over along his length, so each end stays where it is.

When leaving the ceiling:

Cat drop: he lets go, falls, turns over on the way and lands on his feet, the end that hung lowest first. Swing down: he keeps his grip while his body and other limbs swing down, then lets go and drops.

Dropping, roll belly-down:

Like the others: as set above. Drop: he lets go, falls straight down and lands on four legs (head and tail up) or on all six. Rolling: his body rolls half a turn along its length on the way, so he comes down belly first. Not rolling: his body only tilts level (the short way), and the legs that held the ceiling swing over to its underside.

Leaving six legs, stand on:

Back end tips him back onto the end behind him. Front end rises forward over his front legs, so he ends up the other way up, looking exactly the same but with the paw colours swapped. End A / End B pick a paw colour, whichever end it is on.
